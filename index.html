/ Our story

Built by people who needed a gym like this

Forge Fitness started in 2019 with one floor, a handful of racks and a simple idea: a gym should feel welcoming on day one. Too many people quit because they felt lost or watched. So we hired coaches who teach, kept the floor uncrowded and built plans around real schedules.

Our mission: help every member get stronger, move better and stay consistent for the long run.
